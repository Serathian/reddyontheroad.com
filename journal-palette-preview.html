Design Comparison

❌ Coffee Palette (Before)
  • Yellowish cream background (#FEF3C7)
  • Too bright and warm
  • Abril Fatface — too formal/rigid
  • Merriweather — lacks personality
  • Limited texture/tactile feel
✅ Leather Journal (After)
  • Aged paper neutral (#F5F1E8)
  • Rich leather browns (#3E2723-#8D6E63)
  • Caveat — handwritten warmth
  • Crimson Text — classic journal serif
  • Paper grain & leather textures
  • Vintage photo treatments
  • Weathered, diary-like authenticity
