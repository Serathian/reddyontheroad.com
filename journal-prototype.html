12 Weeks, 3 Countries, Endless Memories

Summer 2022 • Family Motorhome Adventure

In the summer of 2022, we packed up our motorhome and set off on the adventure of a lifetime—a 12-week journey from Finland to Spain and back, exploring the heart of Europe with our 3-year-old daughter. This is our story, one week at a time.

Week 01

The Journey Begins

June 1-7, 2022
📍 Helsinki → Turku → Naantali
📸
Ready to roll!
📸
First stop
📸
Excited faces

Our adventure kicked off in Helsinki on a bright June morning. The motorhome was packed to the brim—clothes, toys, snacks, and endless optimism. Our first destination was Turku, Finland's old capital, where cobblestone streets and medieval architecture greeted us. Our daughter's eyes lit up at every castle and harbor boat.

"Daddy, are we going to drive ALL the way to Spain?"
— Our 3-year-old, Day 1

We spent our evenings in Naantali, parked by the sea, watching the sunset paint the sky in shades of orange and pink. The freedom of the open road was already working its magic.

Week 02

Into Sweden

June 8-14, 2022
📍 Stockholm → Uppsala → Dalarna
📸
Stockholm harbor
📸
Gamla Stan magic
📸
Swedish meatballs!

Crossing into Sweden felt like entering a fairytale. Stockholm's archipelago stretched endlessly, and we spent hours exploring Gamla Stan's narrow alleys and colorful buildings. Our daughter insisted on trying "real Swedish meatballs" at every restaurant—a request we happily obliged.

"This place looks like a toy town!"
— Exploring Gamla Stan

Heading north to Uppsala and Dalarna, we discovered Sweden's lake country— crystal clear waters, red cottages, and endless forests. We swam in lakes so clean you could see the bottom, and our daughter collected pinecones like treasures.

Week 03

Through Denmark

June 15-21, 2022
📍 Copenhagen → Odense → Billund
📸
Nyhavn colors
📸
Little Mermaid
📸
LEGOLAND!

Denmark welcomed us with hygge vibes and bicycle-filled streets. Copenhagen's Nyhavn was even more colorful in person, and we spent an afternoon cruising the canals. But the real highlight? LEGOLAND in Billund—a dream come true for our LEGO-obsessed daughter.

"Can we live here? In the LEGO house?"
— At LEGOLAND, obviously

The Danish countryside surprised us with its rolling hills and thatched-roof villages. We ate smørrebrød, fed ducks in village ponds, and learned that Danish pastries really do taste better in Denmark.
