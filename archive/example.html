Finland → Spain and back

May 15, 2022

Rovaniemi, Finland

Our journey began in the magical city of Rovaniemi, where the Arctic Circle meets adventure. Emma was thrilled to see her first reindeer, and we spent the afternoon exploring the Santa Claus Village. The midnight sun cast a golden glow over everything, making it feel like time stood still.

"Look Daddy, real reindeer! Can we take one home?" - Emma

May 18, 2022

Stockholm, Sweden

Three days later, we arrived in beautiful Stockholm. The archipelago was absolutely stunning, and we found a perfect camping spot overlooking the water. Emma loved feeding the ducks by the harbor, and we enjoyed traditional Swedish fika at a cozy café in Gamla Stan.

"Mommy, can we live here forever?" - Emma

May 25, 2022

Copenhagen, Denmark

Copenhagen welcomed us with its famous hygge atmosphere. We visited Tivoli Gardens, where Emma went on her first roller coaster (the gentle one!). The colorful Nyhavn harbor was the perfect backdrop for family photos, and we enjoyed the best pastries of our lives at a local bakery.

"The merry-go-round was SO FAST!" - Emma (it wasn't)

About This Journey

In the summer of 2022, we embarked on the adventure of a lifetime. With our trusty motorhome packed and our 3-year-old daughter Emma full of excitement, we drove from the Arctic Circle in Finland all the way to the sunny beaches of Spain, and back again.

Over 10,000 kilometers, 12 countries, and countless memories later, we created this digital diary to preserve and share our journey. Every photo, every story, and every moment captured here is a piece of our family's adventure.

"Not all who wander are lost" - J.R.R. Tolkien
