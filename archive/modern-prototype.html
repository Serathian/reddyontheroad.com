Arctic Circle to Helsinki

Rovaniemi → Oulu → Helsinki

Our journey began in the magical city of Rovaniemi, where the Arctic Circle meets adventure. Emma was thrilled to see her first reindeer at the Santa Claus Village. The midnight sun cast a golden glow over everything, making time feel endless. We drove through endless forests and past crystal-clear lakes, stopping in Oulu for traditional Finnish salmon soup before reaching the vibrant capital of Helsinki.

Through Sweden

Stockholm → Gothenburg

Three days exploring Stockholm's beautiful archipelago and Gamla Stan's cobblestone streets. Emma loved feeding the ducks by the harbor, and we discovered the joy of Swedish fika culture. The drive to Gothenburg took us through endless forests and past hundreds of red cottages. We found the perfect camping spot overlooking the North Sea, where Emma collected shells for an entire afternoon.

📍 2 stops 🚗 470 km 📸 42 photos

Danish Hygge

Copenhagen → Aarhus

Copenhagen welcomed us with its famous hygge atmosphere and colorful Nyhavn harbor. Emma's first roller coaster at Tivoli Gardens (the gentle one!) was a huge success. We enjoyed the best pastries of our lives at a cozy bakery, where the baker gave Emma a free cookie every morning. The coastal drive to Aarhus revealed stunning beaches and charming fishing villages where we stopped for fresh seafood.

📍 2 stops 🚗 315 km 📸 56 photos
